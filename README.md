# regions

A simple pixel platformer that is harder than it seems.
<img width="846" height="480" alt="Screen Recording 2026-08-23 at 4 39 39 PM (online-video-cutter com) (2)" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/50eb573f-cbb5-4197-ba62-656a1a5f2ff3" />

You can try it in your browser right *here*: https://deleteware.itch.io/regions
Use WASD or arrows keys to move, and space or W to jump.


Regions includes many features like
 - plug and play in your browser
 - various enemy types
 - challenging obstacles
 - a total of 47 coins to collect, some of which are hidden in secretive, extra hard areas
 - accessible keyboard controls
